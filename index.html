About Our Villa

Experience the perfect blend of Sri Lankan hospitality and modern luxury at Cinnamon Hikka Villa.

Relax, Explore & Enjoy Hikkaduwa

Cinnamon Hikka Villa is a comfortable and peaceful private villa located in the beautiful coastal paradise of Hikkaduwa, Sri Lanka. Offering modern facilities and a relaxing atmosphere, the villa is an ideal getaway for families, couples, and travelers looking for a memorable beachside experience.

Located close to popular attractions such as Hikkaduwa Beach, Galle Fort, Madu River, Sinigama Devalaya, Turtle Beach, and beautiful coral reefs, guests can experience the best of Sri Lanka's southern coast. Visitors can enjoy exciting activities including kayaking, boat safaris, snorkeling, coral reef exploration, turtle watching, and beach adventures.

Whether you are seeking relaxation, adventure, or cultural experiences, Cinnamon Hikka Villa provides the perfect base to explore Hikkaduwa.
